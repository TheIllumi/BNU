## The Design Thinking Process

The Design Thinking Process consists of five stages:

1. Empathize
2. Define
3. Ideate
4. Prototype
5. Test

---

## Stage 1: Empathize

> "To create meaningful innovations, you need to know your users and care about their lives."

---

## Stage 2: Define

> "Framing the right problem is the only way to create the right solution."

---

## Stage 3: Ideate

> "It's not about coming up with the 'right' idea, it's about generating the broadest range of possibilities."

---

## Stage 4: Prototype

> "Build to think and test to learn."

---

## Stage 5: Test

> "Testing is an opportunity to learn about your solution and your user."

---

## Design

Six core principles of Design Thinking:

1. **Bias toward action** -- Favor doing and making over prolonged discussion.
2. **Collaborate across boundaries** -- Bring together people from different disciplines and perspectives.
3. **Focus on human values** -- Keep the needs and experiences of people at the center of the design.
4. **Be mindful of process** -- Stay aware of where you are in the design process and what stage demands.
5. **Prototype toward a solution** -- Use prototypes as a thinking and learning tool, not just a presentation artifact.
6. **Show don't tell** -- Communicate ideas visually and tangibly rather than through words alone.
